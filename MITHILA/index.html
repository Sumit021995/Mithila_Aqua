Advanced Technology

The 4-Step Purity & Safety Process

Every drop of Mithila Aqua undergoes rigorous purification, filtration, and safety checks before reaching your table.

1. Micron Filtered

Multi-stage micron filtration removes suspended impurities, dust particles, and turbidity for crystal-clear water.

2. R.O. Processed

Advanced Reverse Osmosis (R.O.) technology eliminates dissolved salts, heavy metals, and unwanted contaminants.

3. UV Treated

Ultraviolet (UV) radiation disinfection neutralizes remaining microorganisms and bacteria instantly and safely.

4. Ozonized

Ozonation locks in absolute freshness, sterilizes the bottled environment, and keeps water crisp and pure for longer.

Storage & Handling Instructions
